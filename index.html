About the Show

Knowledge Fight is an independently produced podcast that chronicles and analyzes the output of Alex Jones — radio host, conspiracy theorist, and founder of InfoWars. Since launching in 2017, hosts Dan Friesen and Jordan Holmes have worked through thousands of hours of Jones's broadcasts, documenting lies, contradictions, grift, and the occasional moment of unintentional comedy.

What sets the show apart is its rigor. Dan and Jordan don't just mock — they catalog. Every episode is meticulously timestamped, fact-checked, and cross-referenced. The result is an unparalleled archive of one of the most influential and dangerous voices in American media.

The show has been cited by journalists, researchers, and lawyers — including during litigation against Jones — as a reliable primary source on his history of statements. It is both a public service and genuinely hilarious radio.

The Hosts

Two Chicagoans who decided someone had to watch all of this.

DF

Dan Friesen

Co-host & Primary Researcher

Dan does the heavy lifting — he watches the InfoWars broadcasts, timestamps the moments, and leads the factual deconstruction. Known for his measured tone, encyclopedic memory for Jones's past statements, and an ability to stay composed while reviewing genuinely unhinged content. He is the bedrock of the show's credibility.

  • Primary episode researcher
  • Keeps meticulous notes
  • The straight man of the duo
JH

Jordan Holmes

Co-host & Comic Foil

Jordan brings the irreverence. His reactions to Dan's findings — equal parts disbelief and laughter — give the show its comic engine. He also contributes deep contextual knowledge and, when needed, sharp media criticism. Jordan's willingness to follow Dan down any rabbit hole is what makes the format work.

  • Contextual commentary
  • Genuine comedic instincts
  • Enthusiastic collaborator

Notable Episodes

A few places to start if you're new to the show.

Starter Episode

Episode #1 — The Beginning

Where it all started. Dan and Jordan lay out their methodology and begin working through the InfoWars archives. Essential listening to understand the show's approach and scope.

Fan Favorite

The Sandy Hook Coverage

A landmark series documenting Jones's years of harassment of Sandy Hook families — work that became part of the evidentiary record in subsequent defamation litigation.

Deep Cut

The Supplements Saga

Dan and Jordan's ongoing examination of InfoWars's supplement business — the pricing, the claims, and the extraordinary gap between what Jones says and what the products do.

Milestone

Episode #500

A milestone episode reflecting on half a thousand installments of the show, the state of the InfoWars empire, and what it means to spend years chronicling one man's media output.

Essential

The COVID Era Episodes

Jones's pandemic coverage produced some of his most dangerous misinformation. Dan and Jordan's documentation of this period is essential media criticism in real time.

Recommended

The Trial Coverage

Live and archival coverage of the defamation trials brought by Sandy Hook families. The hosts provide invaluable context that mainstream outlets largely missed.
